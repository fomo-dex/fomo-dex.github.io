Method

Compliance, decomposed by direction

A task-frame splits contact force into task-relevant directions. Each direction gets its own correction law, so the robot yields where it should and stays precise everywhere else.

▼

Surface Contact Retreat

When a fingertip presses too hard on a supporting surface, the finger retreats proportional to the excess force — protecting the object and the grasp.

Directional Tool Compliance

A mass–spring–damper law absorbs sideways force on a moving tool while preserving motion along its intended axis.

Rotational Force Regulation

A PI loop holds fingertip normal force near a target during screwdriving, keeping a stable grasp around the rotation axis.
